Music Tips

How to Maintain Your Instrument's Finish

+

Use a microfiber cloth for daily cleaning. Avoid silicone-based polishes on nitrocellulose finishes. Store in a case with stable humidity between 45-55%. Never leave your instrument in direct sunlight or near heating vents.

String Changing Best Practices

+

Change strings one at a time to maintain neck tension. Stretch new strings gently after installation. Wipe down strings after each session to extend their life. Consider coated strings for humid climates.

Humidity Control for Wooden Instruments

+

Wood expands and contracts with humidity changes. Use a digital hygrometer in your case. In dry climates, use a dampit or case humidifier. In humid climates, silica gel packs can help absorb excess moisture.

When to Schedule Professional Setup

+

Schedule a professional setup when changing string gauges, after seasonal humidity shifts, if you notice fret buzz, or annually for instruments in regular use. A proper setup transforms playability.
