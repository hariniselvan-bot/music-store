Restoration

Repair & Restoration

Before After

Our restoration experts can bring even the most damaged instruments back to life. From crack repairs to full refinishing, we treat every piece with the care it deserves.

Setup

Professional Setup

1

Inspection

Comprehensive evaluation of neck relief, fret condition, hardware, and electronics. We document every detail before beginning work.

2

Neck Adjustment

Precise truss rod adjustment to achieve optimal neck relief. We use digital measurement tools for accuracy within thousandths of an inch.

3

Fret Work

Leveling, crowning, and polishing to eliminate buzz and ensure perfect intonation across the entire fretboard.

4

Final Calibration

String height optimization, intonation adjustment at the bridge, and pickup height balancing for optimal tone and playability.
