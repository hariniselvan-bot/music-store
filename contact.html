FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a custom build take? +

Custom instruments typically require 8-12 months from initial consultation to delivery, depending on complexity and materials availability.

Do you offer trade-ins? +

Yes, we accept select premium instruments as trade-ins toward new purchases. Each instrument is evaluated by our master luthiers.

Can I try before I buy? +

We offer a 48-hour home trial for customers within the EU. Instruments must be returned in original condition with all packaging.

Do you finance? +

We partner with select financial institutions to offer 0% financing for qualified buyers on purchases over €2,000.
