About MUSICA

Our Story

Where Passion
Meets Precision

Founded in 1985 by master luthier Antonio Rivera, MUSICA began as a small workshop in Milan, Italy. What started as a passion for crafting the perfect violin has evolved into a global brand synonymous with excellence in musical instruments.

Today, our instruments grace the stages of the world's most prestigious concert halls, from La Scala to Carnegie Hall. Every piece that leaves our workshop carries decades of tradition, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to sonic perfection.

Philosophy

We believe that music is the highest form of human expression, and every instrument we create is a vessel for that expression. Our mission is to remove the barriers between the musician and their sound.
